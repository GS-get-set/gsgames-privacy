Overview

Welcome to Puzzle Games Collection, developed by GS Games. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Our app is a collection of brain-teasing puzzle games — Hanoi Tower, Memory Match, Nonogram, and Solitaire — designed for entertainment and mental exercise. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

✅  We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Name, email address, or any contact information
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, serial number, etc.)
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Any personally identifiable information

No account registration or login is required to use the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Game progress and best scores for each game
  • App settings (theme preferences, sound and haptic settings)
  • Puzzle completion history

💾  All local data can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain information to serve relevant ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address (used for general location)
  • App usage data for ad targeting purposes
  • Device information (type, OS version)

📋  For full details on how Google collects and uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob's data practices .

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe your child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us and we will take immediate steps to address it.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. Privacy Policy
  • Expo (React Native) — the framework used to build the app. Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Security

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no user data stored on our servers to protect. All game data is stored locally on your device under the protection of your device's own security measures.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
